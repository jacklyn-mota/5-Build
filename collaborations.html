Collaboration

Connect with other creatives to bring ideas to life.

Independent filmmaking often depends on collaboration. The Indie Film Collective helps connect creative people who want to share ideas, develop projects, and contribute their skills to new productions. Whether you are looking for collaborators for an existing project or hoping to gain experience on someone else's production, the collective provides opportunities to meet other creatives

Contact the Collective

Have a project idea or looking for creative collaborators? We'd love to hear from you. Send us a message with a little information about yourself, your interests, and the type of project you'd like to work on.
